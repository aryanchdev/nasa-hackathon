NASA uses the "RIDGE" framework to classify all spaceflight hazards. If your app targets these, the judges will know you did the actual research.

## 1. The RIDGE Framework

- **Radiation:** The biggest mission killer. Deep space radiation breaks down DNA, causes cancer, and triggers cognitive decline.
    
- **Isolation:** Being stuck in a small metal box ruins sleep, crushes morale, and causes crew friction.
    
- **Distance:** Mars means a 20-minute radio delay each way. Earth cannot help in a medical emergency. The app must be completely offline-first.
    
- **Gravity:** Shifting from Earth (1G) to Space (0G) to Mars (0.38G) destroys bone density, heart function, and physical balance.
    
- **Environment:** Spaceships are closed, loud machines. CO2 builds up easily, and bacteria mutate faster while astronaut immune systems drop.
    

## 2. Hidden Health Problems (The Differentiators)

- **SANS (Vision Loss):** Zero gravity pushes fluids up into the head. This flattens the back of the eyeball and blurs vision over time.
    
- **Artery Aging:** Six months in space stiffens neck arteries, mimicking 10 to 20 years of rapid aging.
    
- **Vestibular Failure:** Floating breaks the inner ear balance system. When astronauts land on a planet, they suffer severe vertigo and struggle to walk straight.
    
- **The Bends & Hypoxia:** Spacewalks involve extreme pressure changes, risking nitrogen bubbles in the blood or low oxygen to tissues.
    
- **Mutant Microbiomes:** Closed cabins speed up bacterial mutation. Stress weakens the immune system, waking up dormant viruses in the body.
    

## 3. NASA Baseline Tracking

Actual data NASA collects. The Workflow :-

- **Actigraphy:** Wearables tracking exact sleep phases and light exposure.
    
- **Cognitive Tests:** Short reaction and memory games to catch space brain fog.
    
- **Biomarker Logging:** Manual input fields for routine blood, urine, and saliva tests.
    
- **Quick Pings:** A morning survey for sleep quality, and an evening survey for mood and team cohesion.
    
- **Sensorimotor Tracking:** Balance and blood pressure checks immediately before and after spacewalks.
    

Ideas from Past related Hackathons and Contest

- **The RIDGE Dashboard:** Instead of a generic health screen, grade the astronaut on the 5 NASA hazards. Show metrics like "Radiation Load Limit" or "Gravity Adaptation Score."
    
- **Morning Fog Check:** A 30-second reaction mini-game when they wake up. If they score 15% slower than their baseline, the app flags poor sleep or high cabin CO2.
    
- **Offline ER Protocol:** A built-in diagnostic decision tree. If an astronaut selects "dizzy and blurry vision," the app immediately flags SANS (Spaceflight associated neuroocular syndrome) and provides offline mitigation steps without waiting for Earth.
    
- **10-Second Vision Check:** A UI prompt asking them to hold the tablet at arm's length and read a line of text. This tracks eyeball flattening over the 6-month journey.
